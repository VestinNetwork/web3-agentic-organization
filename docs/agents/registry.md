# Agent Registry

38 specialist role profiles across six divisions. Roles are capability profiles, not always-on model processes. Spawn instances on demand and bind them to mission/task scopes.

## Executive
Chief Orchestrator, Chief Architect, Chief Security Officer, Chief Operations Officer, Chief Product Officer, Chief Intelligence Officer.

## Strategy and intelligence (6)
Strategy Analyst, Web3 Researcher, Market Analyst, Tokenomics Analyst, Protocol Analyst, Competitive Analyst.

## Blockchain engineering (8)
Blockchain Architect, Solidity Engineer, Solana Engineer, DeFi Engineer, Backend Engineer, Frontend Engineer, Indexer Engineer, DevOps Engineer.

## Security and risk (7)
Security Architect, Smart Contract Auditor, DeFi Auditor, AppSec Engineer, Infrastructure Security, Fuzzing Engineer, Risk Analyst.

## QA and verification (5)
QA Lead, Unit Test Engineer, Integration Tester, E2E Tester, Release Validator.

## Blockchain operations (6)
Chain Monitor, Transaction Analyst, Wallet Monitor, Treasury Analyst, Incident Responder, Governance Monitor.

## Product and growth (6)
Product Manager, UX Researcher, UI Designer, Technical Writer, Ecosystem Analyst, Community Analyst.

Developers cannot approve their own security review. Mission policy defines independent reviewers, evidence and approvals. Chief Orchestrator cannot change policy or authorize its own privileged tool calls.
